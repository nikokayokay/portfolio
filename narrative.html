A Lifelong Connection to Sports

Whether indoors, outdoors, or behind a screen, sports have always been a constant. Growing up playing baseball into his teenage years, while also competing in football, basketball, and shot put — there was always a way to stay involved.

02

Rediscovering the Game Through Media

That connection faded somewhat during high school, but in 2024 it was reignited through Brent Barber and Chris Gloff’s radio program. Joining the conversation around basketball on Beyond the Baseline, along with contributing to sports articles online, renewed both a passion for sports and an interest in covering them from a media and creative perspective.

03

Academic Path & Future Aspirations

Approaching graduation from Crown Point High School with an Academic Honors Diploma, the transition into college is the next chapter. Nikoli will be attending Indiana University, where he is pursuing a degree in Data Sciences with a minor in Economics — a program that aligns directly with the analytical work already underway across sports media, platform management, and broadcast production.

While studying at IU, the plan is to keep building on the applied analytical work already in progress. Moneyballr — an MLB analytics platform combining live data, advanced metrics, and interactive strike zone visualizations — represents exactly the kind of project that sits at the intersection of Data Sciences and sports media. That work will continue to develop alongside the academic program, using the tools and frameworks from the classroom to push the platform further.
