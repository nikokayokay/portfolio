“The CP baseball team returned home Tuesday night for a doubleheader matchup against Michigan City, and the Bulldogs wasted no time taking control. In game one, the Dogs pulled away early behind a dominant outing from senior pitcher Khalin Figurski, who carried a no-hitter into the 4th inning and allowed just one hit on the night, while the offense got going quickly with Caden Matusak launching an RBI homer in the 1st inning and Sean Dunlap adding a solo shot later in the game to help extend the lead the game with 17 runs to none. In game two, Crown Point turned in a complete offensive showcase with a 17-0 win over Michigan City, continuing to pile on runs throughout the night, but the biggest moment came in the 4th inning when Nathan Sheets stole his 28th base of the season to officially break the single-season stolen base record. The Dogs closed out the sweep in dominant fashion as they continue building momentum heading into sectionals.”
